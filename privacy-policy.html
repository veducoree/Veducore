    By using Veducore, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. Please read it carefully.

    Who we are

    Veducore is an all-in-one SaaS platform that helps businesses run their operations — ERP, CRM, billing, HR, inventory, analytics, and white-label web and mobile apps — across industries including education, healthcare, retail, restaurants, fitness, real estate, professional services and manufacturing.

    References to "Veducore", "we", "us" or "our" refer to the Veducore platform and its operators. "Customer" means the business or organisation that subscribes to Veducore, and "End users" means the people whose data a Customer manages on the platform (for example students, patients, members, buyers or employees).

    Information we collect

    Information you provide

    • Account details — name, email address, phone number, business name and role
    • Business records — data your organisation enters into Veducore, such as customer, student, patient, member, employee, inventory, order and invoice records
    • Payment information — billing address and transaction references (we do not store full card details)
    • Communications — messages you send us through email, WhatsApp or our contact and demo forms

    Information collected automatically

    • Usage data — pages visited, features used and time spent on the platform
    • Device information — browser type, operating system, device model and IP address
    • Cookies & local storage — to keep you signed in and remember preferences

    How we use your information

    • Provide, operate, maintain and improve the Veducore platform
    • Process the transactions and records you create (billing, bookings, enrolments, orders and more)
    • Send service notifications, receipts and important updates
    • Respond to support requests and provide onboarding
    • Analyse usage in aggregate to improve the product
    • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents
    • Comply with legal obligations

    We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your business records for advertising.

    Data sharing

    We share information only in these circumstances:

    • Service providers — trusted vendors that help us run the platform (such as cloud hosting, payment processors and messaging providers), bound by confidentiality obligations
    • Legal requirements — when required by law, court order or a government authority
    • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets
    • With your consent — any other sharing happens only with your explicit approval

    Data you manage for your customers

    When a business uses Veducore to manage its own End users' data, that business is the data controller (Data Fiduciary) and decides how the data is used. Veducore acts as a data processor and handles that data only on the business's instructions and to provide the service.

    Some End users may be minors — for example students in schools or coaching institutes. The Customer is responsible for obtaining any consent required from parents or guardians. End users who want to access, correct or delete their data should contact the business they deal with; we will support that business in responding.

    Data retention

    We keep personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion at any time. We delete or anonymise data within 30 days of a valid request, unless we must keep it longer to meet legal, tax or accounting obligations.

    After a subscription ends, Customer data remains available for export for 30 days, after which it may be permanently deleted.

    Data security

    • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
    • Secure cloud infrastructure with access controls
    • Role-based permissions so each team member sees only what they need
    • Regular backups and monitoring
    • Limited employee access on a need-to-know basis

    No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We take all reasonable steps to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you as required by law.

    Cookies

    We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies in your browser settings, though disabling them may affect some features.

    Your rights

    In line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you
    • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
    • Request deletion of your data
    • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
    • Raise a grievance and have it addressed

    To exercise any of these rights, email veducoree@gmail.com.

    Changes to this policy

    We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or a notice on the platform. Continuing to use Veducore after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
