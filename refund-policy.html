    Overview

    We want every business on Veducore to be happy with the platform. Circumstances change, so we've designed this policy to be fair to both sides. It applies to all subscription plans — Starter, Growth and Enterprise — across every industry we serve.

    Refund eligibility

    EligibleYou can get a refund if

    • You ask within 7 days of your first payment and haven't actively used the platform (no records imported, no customers or users added)
    • A technical failure on our side blocks access and we can't fix it within 7 business days
    • You were charged incorrectly because of a billing error on our part
    • What you bought was materially different from what was described at purchase

    Not eligibleRefunds don't apply to

    • Requests made more than 7 days after the first payment
    • Accounts that have been actively used (data added, invoices raised, users onboarded)
    • Partial refunds for unused days within a billing period
    • Change of mind after onboarding has started
    • Add-ons, custom development or one-time setup fees
    • Renewal payments for existing subscriptions
    Annual plans: annual subscription fees are non-refundable after the 7-day window, regardless of usage. We recommend booking a free demo before committing to an annual plan.

    How to request a refund

    1. Email usWrite to veducoree@gmail.com with the subject "Refund Request – [Your Business Name]".
    2. Include detailsYour registered email, payment transaction ID, date of payment and the reason for the request.
    3. We review it2 business daysWe acknowledge your request and check eligibility.
    4. You get a decision5–7 business daysA final decision from the day you submitted the request.
    5. Refund processed7–10 business daysApproved refunds are credited to your original payment method.

    Refund method

    Approved refunds go back to the original payment method:

    • Credit / debit card — refunded to the same card
    • UPI / net banking — refunded to the originating account
    • Bank transfer — refunded to the source bank account

    We don't issue refunds in cash, by cheque or to a different payment method.

    Cancellation

    You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or by contacting support. When you cancel:

    • Your access continues until the end of the current billing period
    • No further charges are made
    • Your data stays available for export for 30 days, after which it may be permanently deleted
    • Cancellation doesn't automatically trigger a refund — see eligibility above

    Disputes

    If you disagree with a refund decision, reply to your original refund email to escalate. We'll re-review within 5 business days and aim to resolve every dispute fairly and promptly.
